— The concept —

Three pillars, one café.

Back to the days of old · Belgian gourmet food · Beer lover's paradise

— Back to the days of old —

The interior

An authentic Belgian café from another era: dark panelled wood, zinc counters with brass rails, globe pendants, chalk beer boards and a Flemish verse on the wall.

— Belgian gourmet food —

The food

Our national pride Belgian fries, sharing hoppetizers, gourmet mussels and more, and famous Belgian desserts: unfussy classics, done properly.

— Beer lover's paradise —

The beer

A curated range of Belgian beers, each in its designated glass, beer pairing on the menu, a beer of the month, and the 9-step pouring ritual every bartender learns.

— The model —

Two fees, one partner.

An entrance fee covers on-site training front and back of house, the operating manuals, recipes and supplier support. A royalty fee covers the ongoing design, marketing and operations support. The figures, and the key performance indicators of the running cafés, are in the franchise pack.
